Athens, from the top deck

A companion for the red Athens Line hop-on hop-off bus (stops A1 to A15, from Syntagma). Short clips for when the bus rolls on, long ones for when you hop off or sit in traffic. Each stop lists what's worth getting off for, with rough times. Places closed today are left off; hours change, so check before you go.

On iPhone, add this page to the Home Screen first, then save from the Home Screen app.
