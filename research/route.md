Earlier note (Athens Line, 15 stops): The
operator's site doesn't publish stop order. The map's arrows run the loop in number order, starting at A1
Syntagma (the terminal, T, is on Amalias just off Syntagma). Buses leave every few minutes from about 08:30;
the last departure is around 18:30 in summer. A full loop takes roughly 90 minutes without stopping. You hear
it from the open top deck, in traffic, with the bus's own commentary in other people's headphones.

Ground rules for narration:
- **Verify every fact you state** (dates, names, numbers) with a quick web search when unsure. This page is public.
- **Don't promise left or right.** Seat, direction and traffic vary. Say "look for" or "up on the rock".
- **Short clip:** about 40 seconds, roughly 450–650 characters, for when the bus rolls straight past.
- **Long clip:** 2–3 minutes, roughly 1,800–2,900 characters, for hopping off or sitting in traffic.
- **No SSML.** Pauses are paragraph breaks or an occasional `[pause]`. No other bracket tags.
- **Greek names:** write them plainly in Latin letters (Syntagma, Monastiraki, Kallimarmaro). The renderer
  swaps in pronunciation.
